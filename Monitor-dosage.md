# How to Monitor Dosage on Welth

## Summary
Learn how to track your medication schedule and intake using the Welth app.

## Who This Is For
This guide is intended for patients and regular users of the Welth app.

## What You Can Do
- View your current medications  
- Monitor dosage and intake times  
- Report side effects to healthcare providers  

## View Your Current Medications

This feature allows you to:
- View a list of all currently prescribed medications  
- See dosage details under each listed medication  

To view your current medications:
1. Go to **Track Medication** on the Welth home screen.
2. Tap the condition type (e.g., *Rheumatoid arthritis*).  
   <img src="https://github.com/Samuelsen1/Few-Important-Pics/blob/main/viewmeds.png?raw=true" alt="Welth app showing current medications by condition" width="300">
3. Tap the type of medication (e.g., *Ibuprofen*).  
   <img src="https://github.com/Samuelsen1/Few-Important-Pics/blob/main/medicsforrheu.png?raw=true" alt="Welth app showing ibuprofen for rheumatoid arthritis" width="300">

> **Note:** Medication duration is automatically tracked.  
> For example, if your doctor prescribes ibuprofen, it appears under your current medications.  
> Once the course is completed, it moves to your *Medication History* automatically.

## Monitor Dosage

This feature allows you to:
- Receive reminder alerts (e.g., 10 minutes before dosage time)  
- Mark medications as "taken" after each dose  
- Manually add or remove medications as needed
