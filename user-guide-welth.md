## 1. Introduction to Welth
**Welth** is a user-friendly platform designed to help users manage health conditions using information from the World Health Organization (WHO). It enables users to:

- Track symptoms  
- Monitor medication intake  
- Book nearby medical appointments  
- Order prescribed medications  
- Manage ongoing health conditions  

Welth is designed for patients, caregivers, doctors, and all users aged 16 and above.

---

## 2. Creating an Account
1. Go to the **Google Play Store** (Android) or **App Store** (iOS).  
2. Install the **Welth** app.  
3. Create an account using a username and password.  
   _Alternatively, tap **Continue with Google** to create your account via Google._  
4. Set your default health condition(s) on the next screen.  

> **Note:** Ensure your condition has been diagnosed by a medical professional. If not, tap **Only Explore** at the bottom to access other features without entering a condition.

## 5. Contacting a Doctor or Caregiver
This feature allows users to:

- Browse a list of verified doctors and caregivers with contact details  
- Add selected professionals to their account  
- Share health data with them directly via the platform  

**To use this feature:**

- Tap **Contact Doctor**  
- Select a professional to view their details

## 6. Purchasing Medications
This feature allows users to:

- Order medications from accredited online pharmacies using a valid prescription  
- Track shipments  
- View reviews for pharmacy shops  

**To use this feature:**

1. Tap **Pharmacy Shop**  
2. Tap **Order**  
3. Upload a valid doctor's prescription containing your health insurance details  

The system will verify the prescription and display a list of available pharmacies.

> If the insurance information is missing, users will be prompted to complete the purchase via PayPal or Visa.

## 8. Settings and Privacy

Users can manage:

- Platform language  
- Payment methods  
- Data sharing preferences  
- Linked doctors or caregivers  
- Data backups

**To manage privacy:**

1. Tap the **Menu** button on the Home screen  
2. Go to **Settings > Privacy**

1. Tap the **Menu** button on the Home screen

## 9. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

- **Can I use the platform to maintain healthy habits even without a diagnosed condition?**  
  Yes. Tap **Only Explore** during account setup to access health tips and tools.

- **Does anyone other than my doctor or caregiver have access to my health data?**  
  No. Not even Welth can access your personal health data.

- **Can I cancel a mistaken medication order?**  
  Yes. Go to **Pharmacy Shop > Cancel Order**.

- **Can I return an order?**  
  Yes. Go to **Pharmacy Shop > Return Order**.  

  _Alternatively, open your confirmation email and tap **Cancel Order** or **Return Order** directly._
