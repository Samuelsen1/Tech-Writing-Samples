How to Monitor Dosage on Welth

Summary

Learn how to track your medication times on Welth.

Who This Is For

Patients and regular users.

What You Can Do

  • View your current medication
  • Monitor dosage
  • Report side effects

View Your Current Medication

This feature enables the user to:

  • View the list of current medications.
  • View the dosage under each medication.

To view your current medication:

    1. Tap Track Medication on the Welth home screen.
    2. Tap View Medication on the next screen.

Note: Each medication duration is automatically set. For instance, when your doctor/caregiver prescribes ibuprofen, the ibuprofen adds to the list of medications until the intake is complete, then the ibuprofen moves from the current list of medication to "Medication History."

Monitor Dosage

This feature enables the user to:

  • Get reminder alerts (e.g., 10 minutes before each dosage)
  • Check off when you take your medicine
  • Manually add and remove medication.
